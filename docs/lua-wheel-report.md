# Lua: `wheelReport.lua`

- **`lua/ge/extensions/wheelReport.lua`** + **`wheel_report.py`** — Every wheel on the driven vehicle and on whatever is coupled to it: position, tire pressure, brake temperature. Port 4485, **send only**. While its packets are fresh (`WHEEL_REPORT_STALE_SEC`) its rows replace the extended struct's six fixed wheel slots on the status screen; when they stop (an older mod half, the extension down) the slot rows come back.
  - **Why the struct could not do it.** The struct's wheel slots are matched by jbeam **name** — `FL FR RL RR F R`. The 0.40 Percheron's wheels are `FL FR R2LL R2RR TLL TLR`, so it showed its front axle and nothing else, and a trailer's wheels live in a different vehicle VM that the protocol never sees. Widening the struct would still have needed names to pick slots.
  - **Labels come from geometry, never names.** Each vehicle VM reports raw longitudinal / lateral position (lateral is `up:cross(fwd)`, positive LEFT), tire width, pressure and brake temperature; `wheel_report.py` does all the grouping, so `diagnostic/wheel_report_sim.py` can assert it. Positions are relative to the vehicle's **origin, which is not on its centreline** (the ATV's wheels arrive at lateral −0.4 and +1.7 m), so each vehicle is centred on the midpoint of its own wheel spread before sides are decided. Wheels within `AXLE_GAP_M` along the vehicle are one axle; two axles read `front` / `rear` (exactly the old slot labels), more number the middle ones (`axle 2`), one axle has no axle word. Two physics wheels on one axle end read `inner` / `outer`, left to right across the vehicle. Coupled vehicles are prefixed `trailer`, numbered only when there is more than one, in hop order from `core_vehicles.attachedCouplers` (the registry `trailerAngle.lua` reads).
  - **Simplified duallies.** Percheron dual rears are, with the jbeam's `simplifiedDuallies` component on, **one** physics wheel 0.59 m wide drawn as two tire meshes — there is no inner and outer tire to read. The vehicle VM sends the switch; a wheel at least `DUAL_WIDTH_RATIO` times the vehicle's narrowest is labelled `dual`.
  - **Unknown is an empty field, not zero.** No pressure group (a solid tire) → no pressure row; `enableBrakeThermals` false → no brake row. A flat tire is a real `0`. A wheel that has broken off (`isBroken`) reads `broken off` in both lists.
  - **No tire temperatures.** Checked on the 0.40 Percheron: no wheel carries a `thermals` table, so there is nothing to report; the struct's tire temperature fields are 0 for the same reason and their status row stays commented out.
  - The vehicle half is a cross-VM chunk (`VEHICLE_CHUNK`): no percent sign and no doubled close-square-bracket in it, which is why it rounds with `math.floor`. The sim greps for both.
  - Diagnostic: `extensions.wheelReport.diag()` in GE prints each vehicle's last raw reply and its age.
