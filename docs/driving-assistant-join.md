# Driving assistant: join stage

Added 2026-10-29. Files: `drivingAssistant.lua`, `roadDetector.lua` (`assistantStart` limits, `assistantJoinTarget`), vehicle `beamtelAssistant.lua` (`join`, `endJoin`). Sim: `diagnostic/driving_assistant_sim.lua`.

Switching on away from a road used to refuse ("No reachable navigable road nearby", or the alignment angle). Now, when `roadDetector.assistantStart` finds no road under the car, `drivingAssistant.lua` enters a **join stage** instead:

- `assistantJoinTarget` picks the nearest edge within 500 m, and its far node in the legal direction (on a two-way road, the end the car already faces). GE says "Off road. AI has full control. Driving to the road, N metres to your left/right/ahead/behind".
- The vehicle's `beamtelAssistant.join` calls stock `ai.driveUsingPath{wpTargetList = {node}, routeSpeed = 10, routeSpeedMode = 'limit'}` with `setRecoverOnCrash(false)` (stock recovery can reset the vehicle). **Nothing is filtered and no input state is saved:** AI has throttle, brake and gears as well as steering. It sends `alive` every 0.2 s and ends on a 3 s heartbeat lease, or if AI drops to `disabled` by itself.
- Every GE tick re-runs `assistantStart(player, 1, 30)`: within 1 m of the road edge and 30 degrees of the legal direction, much stricter than switch-on's 8 m and 80 degrees, so the hand-over happens on the road, not on the verge mid-turn. It then queues the ordinary `start`, whose roadMessage is "On the road. Throttle and brake are yours". `start` accepts the joining token, disables stock AI first (`endJoin`), then takes the steering as before.
- Stage one is `assistantStart(player, 1)`: on the road means within 1 m of its edge (angle still up to 80 degrees). The original 8 m switch-on margin made a car parked on the verge count as on the road; it started assisted steering, went straight into "Rejoining the road", and the join stage never ran. The 8 m margin now applies only when `assistantJoinTarget` is missing.
- Only an `assistantStart` failure joins. A car already on a road whose `assistantUpdate` fails (a roundabout just ahead) still refuses. "Navigation is unavailable" refuses too.
- It gives up after 15 s below 0.5 m/s or 180 s in all: "Could not reach a road. Brake now. Driving assistant off".

**Gearbox mode: stock AI does not restore it here.** `setMode` saves the gearbox mode only when its *previous* mode was `disabled`. But `driveUsingPath` enters through `setState({mode = 'manual'})`, which merges `M.mode` before calling `setMode()`, so the previous mode already reads `manual`. AI switches to arcade without saving, and its disable restores nothing. A driven run that gave up left the player in arcade. So `join` records `electrics.values.gearboxMode` itself, and `endJoin` sets it back after `ai.setMode('disabled')` on every exit: hand-over, toggle off, give-up, AI quitting, lost lease, reset. The sim's join tests use a stock-like mock that does not restore. Its general `ai.setMode` mock always restores realistic, which is how it hid this. Unlike the steering-only start, the restore is not blocked, because the player's mode must come back. With realistic mode and automatic clutch off, a hand-over in first gear drops to neutral (the stock behaviour described under the 2026-09-14 run in [driving-assistant-validation.md](driving-assistant-validation.md)).

`driving_assistant_sim.lua` covers the target choice (side, distance, two-way facing, one-way legality), the strict hand-over test, the vehicle join/hand-over/stop, AI quitting, the lease, and the coordinator's join, hand-over, plan flow and stall give-up. Not yet driven: real stock-AI behaviour from a field, a car facing the wrong way on a one-way road (AI must turn round), and hand-over speed.
